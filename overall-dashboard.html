How tracking works

All practice, exams, visual questions, Indices, mixed mocks, and Medication Matching now feed one progress record on this device. “First try” is counted only once per question.

所有练习、考试、图像题、指数、综合模拟考试及药物配对现在都会记录在此设备上的同一进度档案中。每题仅计算一次“首次作答”。
