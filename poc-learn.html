1

Assessment

评估

Collect subjective and objective data: chief concern, histories, medications, vital signs, extraoral and intraoral findings, periodontal and hard-tissue findings.

NDHCE Pearl
Ask what information could change safety, diagnosis, or planning.

Common trap: Do not begin by choosing treatment. First determine what is happening.

2

Vital Signs

生命体征

Measure and interpret blood pressure, pulse, respiration, and other relevant observations using correct technique.

NDHCE Pearl
Unexpected findings should be verified and acted on according to risk.

Common trap: Dental hygienists assess and refer; they do not diagnose systemic disease.

3

Extraoral & Intraoral Exam

口外与口内检查

Use a systematic sequence so structures are not missed. Describe lesions by site, size, colour, surface, border, consistency, symptoms, and duration.

NDHCE Pearl
Persistent or suspicious findings require documentation and timely referral.

Common trap: “Looks unusual” is not adequate documentation.

4

Periodontal Assessment

牙周评估

Combine probing depths, bleeding, recession, attachment level, mobility, furcation, deposits, inflammation, radiographs, and risk factors.

NDHCE Pearl
Interpret the pattern, not one isolated number.

Common trap: Pocket depth alone does not equal attachment loss.

5

Risk Assessment

风险评估

Identify disease indicators, modifying factors, protective factors, behaviours, and social barriers that change probability or prognosis.

NDHCE Pearl
Risk helps determine priority, prevention, and interval.

Common trap: A risk factor is not automatically a diagnosis.

6

Dental Hygiene Diagnosis

牙科卫生诊断

Organize assessment evidence into client needs and responses that dental hygiene care can address.

NDHCE Pearl
Link the problem to contributing factors and evidence.

Common trap: Do not substitute a medical or dental diagnosis outside scope.

7

Planning

计划

Prioritize needs, establish measurable outcomes, select evidence-informed interventions, sequence care, and collaborate with the client.

NDHCE Pearl
Goals should be specific and measurable.

Common trap: A plan without client participation may be clinically elegant but practically unusable.

8

Informed Consent

知情同意

Explain the proposed care, material risks, benefits, alternatives, and likely consequences of declining. Confirm capacity and voluntariness.

NDHCE Pearl
Consent is an ongoing conversation.

Common trap: A signature alone does not prove meaningful consent.

9

Implementation

实施

Deliver care safely, adapt to the client's current condition, use infection prevention, provide education, and monitor response throughout the visit.

NDHCE Pearl
Reassess whenever the situation changes.

Common trap: The plan may need modification during care.

10

Evaluation & Documentation

评价与记录

Compare outcomes with baseline and goals, determine whether goals were met, revise the plan, and record findings and decisions objectively.

NDHCE Pearl
Document findings, care, response, consent, referrals, and follow-up.

Common trap: Documentation belongs throughout the process, not only at the end.
