Core Principles

核心原理

Pharmacokinetics describes what the body does to a drug: absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretion. Pharmacodynamics describes what the drug does to the body.

NDHCE Pearl
Separate potency from efficacy and first-pass metabolism from bioavailability.
2

Adverse Reactions & Allergies

不良反应与过敏

Distinguish predictable dose-related effects, idiosyncratic reactions, intolerance, toxicity, and immune-mediated allergy.

NDHCE Pearl
Hives plus breathing difficulty is an emergency, not a routine side effect.
3

Autonomic Drugs

自主神经药物

Sympathetic and parasympathetic effects explain many cardiovascular, respiratory, and salivary responses.

NDHCE Pearl
Anticholinergic burden often appears in the mouth as xerostomia.
4

Cardiovascular Drugs

心血管药物

Know beta blockers, ACE inhibitors, calcium channel blockers, nitrates, diuretics, and dental implications.

NDHCE Pearl
Dry cough, gingival enlargement, orthostatic hypotension, and vasoconstrictor interactions are classic clues.
5

Anticoagulants & Antiplatelets

抗凝药与抗血小板药

Assess bleeding risk without casually interrupting medication. Warfarin is monitored with INR; aspirin alters platelet function.

NDHCE Pearl
Do not independently tell a client to stop therapy.
6

Analgesics & Opioids

镇痛药与阿片类

Compare acetaminophen, NSAIDs, and opioids by mechanism, benefits, and major toxicities.

NDHCE Pearl
Acetaminophen: liver. NSAIDs: stomach, kidneys, bleeding. Opioids: breathing and consciousness.
7

Antibiotics

抗生素

Recognize penicillins, cephalosporins, macrolides, tetracyclines, clindamycin, and metronidazole, plus stewardship principles.

NDHCE Pearl
Clarify the allergy reaction and watch for C. difficile and resistance.
8

Antifungals & Antivirals

抗真菌与抗病毒药

Nystatin treats oral candidiasis; acyclovir-family drugs suppress herpes replication.

NDHCE Pearl
Antivirals suppress active replication but do not remove latent virus.
9

Respiratory & Diabetes Drugs

呼吸与糖尿病药物

Rescue inhalers rapidly relieve bronchospasm; inhaled steroids may predispose to candidiasis. Insulin and some diabetes drugs can cause hypoglycemia.

NDHCE Pearl
Frequent rescue inhaler use and hypoglycemia symptoms change dental risk immediately.
10

Corticosteroids & Bone Drugs

糖皮质激素与骨代谢药物

Long-term corticosteroids affect adrenal function, healing, infection, and bone. Potent antiresorptives may be associated with MRONJ.

NDHCE Pearl
Prevention and pretreatment dental stabilization matter.
11

Local Anesthetics

局部麻醉药

Local anesthetics block voltage-gated sodium channels. Vasoconstrictors prolong action but require patient-specific assessment.

NDHCE Pearl
Know early systemic toxicity signs and calculate total dose carefully.
12

Psychotropic Drugs & Polypharmacy

精神科药物与多重用药

Many psychotropic and anticholinergic drugs cause xerostomia, sedation, orthostatic effects, or interactions.

NDHCE Pearl
Medication reconciliation includes prescriptions, OTC products, herbals, cannabis, and alcohol.
13

Emergency Medications

急救药物

Know the purpose of epinephrine, nitroglycerin, salbutamol, glucose, oxygen, and naloxone.

NDHCE Pearl
Match the drug to the emergency and prioritize airway, breathing, and circulation.
