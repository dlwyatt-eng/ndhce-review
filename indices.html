Study Cards 学习卡

High-yield comparisons 高频易混淆比较

PairMain differenceBoard trap
DMFT vs DMFS按牙 vs 按牙面DMFT counts teeth; DMFS counts surfaces and is more sensitive.S means surfaces.
DMFT vs deft恒牙 vs 乳牙DMFT is permanent dentition; deft is primary dentition.Primary teeth use e because normal exfoliation complicates “missing.”
PCR vs Plaque Index有无 vs 厚度PCR is a percentage of positive surfaces; PI grades plaque thickness 0–3.Do not convert a PI average into a PCR percentage.
PHP vs OHI-S五区菌斑 vs 软垢加牙石PHP divides selected surfaces into five plaque areas; OHI-S combines debris and calculus on six surfaces.OHI-S is not plaque alone.
GI vs GBI炎症程度 vs 出血百分比GI grades inflammation 0–3; GBI records the percentage of sites bleeding.GI is not simply a bleeding yes/no record.
GBI vs PBI有无百分比 vs 龈乳头分级GBI is commonly dichotomous; PBI grades papillary bleeding severity.PBI focuses on interdental papillae.
PSR vs full charting筛查 vs 完整检查PSR records one highest code per sextant; comprehensive charting records site-level findings.PSR does not replace a complete periodontal examination when disease is suspected.
