Caries

DMFT

Permanent dentition: Decayed, Missing due to caries, and Filled Teeth. It measures cumulative caries experience.恒牙龋失补牙指数，反映累积龋病经历。

NDHCE trap
A tooth is counted once. Trauma, orthodontic extraction, and congenital absence do not count as M.同一颗牙只计一次；外伤、正畸拔牙和先天缺失不计入M。
Caries

DMFS

Counts affected tooth surfaces rather than teeth, making it more sensitive than DMFT.按牙面计数，比DMFT更敏感。

NDHCE trap
Posterior teeth usually contribute five surfaces; anterior teeth four, depending on the convention used.后牙通常按5个牙面、前牙按4个牙面计算，需遵循题目规则。
Caries

deft

Primary dentition: decayed, indicated for extraction, and filled teeth.乳牙龋、需拔除和充填牙指数。

NDHCE trap
Use e rather than m because primary teeth may be missing from normal exfoliation.乳牙使用e而不是m，因为缺失可能来自正常脱落。
Plaque

PCR

Percentage of examined surfaces with plaque present.有菌斑牙面占全部检查牙面的百分比。

NDHCE trap
PCR records presence or absence, not plaque thickness.PCR记录有无菌斑，不评估厚度。
Plaque

Plaque Index

Silness-Löe index grades plaque thickness from 0 to 3.Silness-Löe菌斑指数按0至3分评估菌斑厚度。

NDHCE trap
A thin film detected only with a probe or disclosing agent is score 1.仅用探针或染色剂发现的薄膜为1分。
Plaque

PHP

Patient Hygiene Performance divides selected tooth surfaces into five areas and records plaque in each area.患者口腔卫生表现指数将选定牙面分为五区记录菌斑。

NDHCE trap
PHP emphasizes distribution of plaque across five surface subdivisions.PHP强调菌斑在五个牙面区域的分布。
Plaque

OHI-S

Simplified Oral Hygiene Index equals DI-S plus CI-S on six selected surfaces.简化口腔卫生指数＝软垢指数DI-S＋牙石指数CI-S。

NDHCE trap
It measures debris and calculus, not plaque alone.它评估软垢和牙石，不仅是菌斑。
Gingival

GI

Löe-Silness Gingival Index grades inflammation from 0 to 3.Löe-Silness牙龈指数按0至3分评估炎症。

NDHCE trap
GI assesses severity of inflammation, not attachment loss.GI评估炎症程度，不评估附着丧失。
Gingival

GBI

Percentage of examined gingival sites that bleed after gentle probing.轻探后出血牙龈位点所占百分比。

NDHCE trap
GBI is generally presence/absence of bleeding; it is not the same as graded PBI severity.GBI通常记录出血有无，不等同于PBI分级严重度。
Gingival

PBI

Papillary Bleeding Index grades the severity of bleeding from interdental papillae.龈乳头出血指数评估邻间龈乳头出血严重度。

NDHCE trap
The key clue is papilla and graded bleeding intensity.关键词是龈乳头及出血程度分级。
Periodontal

PSR

Periodontal Screening and Recording gives the highest code in each sextant.牙周筛查记录每个区段的最高代码。

NDHCE trap
PSR is a screening tool, not a substitute for full periodontal charting when disease is suspected.PSR是筛查工具，发现问题后不能替代完整牙周检查。
Fluorosis

Dean's Index

Classifies dental fluorosis severity based on the two most affected teeth.根据受影响最严重的两颗牙评估氟斑牙严重度。

NDHCE trap
It measures fluorosis, not caries or plaque.评估氟斑牙，不评估龋病或菌斑。
