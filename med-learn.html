Emergency foundations

紧急情况基础

P-A-B-C-D sequence
Stop treatment, position safely, assess responsiveness, activate help, assess breathing, begin CPR/AED care when indicated.停止治疗并确保体位安全，评估反应，启动求助，检查呼吸，并在需要时开始心肺复苏和使用 AED。

Emergency kit readiness
Know where oxygen, epinephrine, nitroglycerin, bronchodilator, glucose, aspirin, AED, suction and emergency numbers are located before care begins.治疗开始前应熟悉氧气、肾上腺素、硝酸甘油、支气管扩张剂、葡萄糖、阿司匹林、AED、吸引设备及急救电话的位置。

Documentation
After the patient is stabilized, document observations, vital signs, interventions, medication dose/route/time, response, EMS activation and disposition.患者稳定后，记录观察结果、生命体征、干预措施、药物剂量/途径/时间、患者反应、EMS 启动情况及后续处置。

2

Basic life support

基础生命支持

Unresponsive patient
Check responsiveness and breathing. If the patient is not breathing normally, call 911, obtain the AED and begin high-quality CPR.检查反应和呼吸。若患者无正常呼吸，立即拨打 911、取得 AED，并开始高质量 CPR。

Adult CPR
Use 30 compressions to 2 breaths, compressing the centre of the chest about 5 cm at 100-120/min, allowing full recoil and minimizing pauses.成人 CPR 采用 30 次按压配 2 次人工呼吸，胸部中央按压约 5 厘米，频率每分钟 100-120 次，充分回弹并尽量减少中断。

Recovery position
Use for an unconscious patient who is breathing normally and has no suspected spinal injury. Continue monitoring until EMS arrives.适用于无意识但呼吸正常且无脊柱损伤嫌疑的患者。保持监测直至 EMS 到达。

Cardiovascular emergencies

心血管急症

Syncope
Stop treatment, place the patient supine with legs elevated, maintain the airway, loosen restrictive clothing, give oxygen if needed and monitor vital signs.停止治疗，使患者仰卧并抬高双腿，维持气道，松开紧身衣物，必要时给氧并监测生命体征。

Angina
Stop treatment, place comfortably, give the patient's nitroglycerin if prescribed, provide oxygen as indicated and monitor. Persistent pain requires EMS activation.停止治疗，调整舒适体位，按处方给予患者自带硝酸甘油，必要时给氧并监测。疼痛持续应启动 EMS。

Myocardial infarction
Call 911 immediately, keep the patient at rest, give oxygen when indicated, consider chewable aspirin if not contraindicated, and be ready for CPR/AED.立即拨打 911，使患者静息，必要时给氧；如无禁忌可考虑嚼服阿司匹林，并准备 CPR/AED。

Hypertensive crisis
Severely elevated blood pressure with symptoms such as chest pain, neurologic change or dyspnea is an emergency. Stop care and activate EMS.血压严重升高并伴胸痛、神经功能改变或呼吸困难属于急症。应停止治疗并启动 EMS。

4

Respiratory emergencies

呼吸系统急症

Asthma
Sit the patient upright, stop treatment, assist with their short-acting bronchodilator, provide oxygen and call EMS if symptoms do not improve promptly.让患者坐直，停止治疗，协助使用短效支气管扩张剂，给予氧气；若症状未迅速改善，应拨打 911。

Hyperventilation
Reassure the patient and coach slow controlled breathing. Do not use a paper bag because hypoxia or another emergency may be mistaken for anxiety.安抚患者并指导缓慢、有控制的呼吸。不要使用纸袋，因为缺氧或其他急症可能被误认为焦虑。

Airway obstruction
If the patient can cough or speak, encourage coughing. If severe obstruction develops, provide age-appropriate thrusts and activate EMS.若患者能咳嗽或说话，应鼓励继续咳嗽。若出现严重气道阻塞，应实施适龄的冲击法并启动 EMS。

Neurologic emergencies

神经系统急症

Seizure
Remove instruments, lower the chair, protect the patient from injury and do not restrain or place anything in the mouth. Afterward, assess breathing and recovery.移除器械、降低牙椅、保护患者免受伤害；不要强行约束，也不要把任何物品放入口中。发作后评估呼吸与恢复情况。

Stroke
Use FAST: facial droop, arm weakness, speech difficulty, time to call 911. Note the time symptoms began and do not give food, drink or medication by mouth.使用 FAST：面部下垂、手臂无力、言语困难、立即拨打 911。记录症状开始时间，不要口服食物、饮料或药物。

Allergy and anaphylaxis

过敏与过敏性休克

Mild allergy
Stop exposure, assess progression and monitor. An antihistamine may help mild isolated symptoms, but it does not replace epinephrine for anaphylaxis.停止接触过敏原，评估进展并监测。抗组胺药可帮助轻度局限症状，但不能替代肾上腺素治疗过敏性休克。

Anaphylaxis
Give intramuscular epinephrine into the anterolateral thigh immediately, call 911, position appropriately, provide oxygen and repeat epinephrine according to protocol if needed.立即在大腿前外侧肌内注射肾上腺素，拨打 911，调整合适体位并给氧；如有需要，按急救方案重复给药。

Diabetic emergencies

糖尿病急症

Hypoglycemia
An alert patient should receive fast-acting oral glucose. If swallowing is unsafe or consciousness is impaired, call EMS and use glucagon according to training/protocol.清醒患者应给予速效口服葡萄糖。若吞咽不安全或意识受损，应启动 EMS，并按培训/方案使用胰高血糖素。

Hyperglycemia
Usually develops more slowly and may include thirst, frequent urination, dry skin and altered consciousness. Stop treatment and seek medical assessment; do not give insulin unless specifically directed.通常进展较慢，可见口渴、尿频、皮肤干燥和意识改变。停止治疗并寻求医疗评估；除非有明确医嘱，不要自行给予胰岛素。

Dental-office complications

牙科诊所并发症

Local anesthetic toxicity
Stop injection, call for help, maintain airway and oxygenation, monitor vital signs, manage seizures and activate EMS for significant symptoms.停止注射，呼叫帮助，维持气道与氧合，监测生命体征，处理惊厥；若症状明显则启动 EMS。

Postoperative bleeding
Apply firm direct pressure with gauze, assess medications and medical history, and refer urgently if bleeding does not stop or the patient becomes unstable.用纱布持续直接加压，评估用药与病史；若出血不止或患者不稳定，应紧急转诊。

Avulsed permanent tooth
Handle by the crown, avoid touching the root, gently rinse if dirty, replant when appropriate or store in a suitable medium, and arrange immediate dental care.握住牙冠，避免触碰牙根；若污染可轻轻冲洗，条件合适时再植或置于合适保存液中，并立即安排牙科治疗。
